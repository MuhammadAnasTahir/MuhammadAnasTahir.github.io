Genomic Mechanisms of Thermal Adaptation in Arctic Pseudomonas

Supervisor: Dr. Huansheng Cao, Assistant Professor of Environmental Science at Duke Kunshan University.

  • Analyzed whole-genome sequencing (WGS) data from 4 Arctic Pseudomonas species evolved under heat stress for 500 generations, identifying SNPs, InDels, structural variants (SVs), and copy number variants (CNVs) across 6 sequenced lineages using GATK, DELLY, CNVnator, and ANNOVAR.
  • Built and ran a multi-stage variant-calling pipeline covering raw read processing, alignment quality control, variant filtering by depth and quality score, and functional annotation mapped to genomic regions (exonic, intronic, intergenic, UTR).
  • Identified 8 genes mutated in common across independent species, including lipopolysaccharide transport gene lptG and ribosomal protein gene rpsJ, pointing to shared genomic targets of thermal selection, and detected a cross-species structural variant duplication at the 1.85–1.99 Mb genomic region.
  • Separated convergent mutations (shared across 2+ species) from lineage-specific ones, and developed Python scripts to filter, cross-reference, and interpret variant data across 20+ sample files.

Aitbaar (Trust)

Top 25 (25/1,280+ teams across Pakistan) — UBL National Innovation Hackathon 2026

National Incubation Center Karachi | United Bank Limited

  • Aitbaar is a two-sided AI credit platform for SME lending in Pakistan: applicants apply over WhatsApp in Urdu, and loan officers decide from a one-page explainable brief.
  • Applicants send photos of their CNIC, bank statement and utility bills, consent once, and get the full document checklist up front — zero branch visits to apply.
  • Five-stage AI engine: Extract (Gemini vision) → Verify (deterministic fraud-flag rules) → Score (XGBoost) → Explain (SHAP factor attribution) → Brief (LLM writes from the SHAP factors only, never decides).
  • Turns a 40 to 60 page loan file into a one-page brief with a score, risk tier, fraud flags and a recommended loan range, processed in under 60 seconds per application.
  • Consent-first and fully auditable: every decision is logged with its reasoning, the applicant always consents before processing, and the officer always makes the final call.
Python FastAPI React Tailwind Node.js whatsapp-web.js Gemini Vision Supabase Railway Vercel

Baseerat (Insight)

Voice AI Hackathon — Uplift AI (YC S25) × Replit x Lahore University of Management Sciences

  • Designed and deployed Baseerat, an Urdu voice agent that lets blind people check money, medicine, and documents using only their phone camera. Voice in, voice out, fully in Urdu.
  • Shutterless camera pipeline: every frame is triaged on the device for sharpness and brightness, and the agent talks the user into position and decides for itself when it has a frame worth answering from, so there is no photo to take.
  • Whole-screen navigation modeled on TalkBack: single tap reads an option aloud, double tap activates it, triple tap goes home, with no menus, no reading, and nothing to aim at.
  • Checks scanned medicine against the user's saved prescription and speaks a dose-mismatch warning before the answer, and in document mode it explains the document to the user.
  • Scene mode describes what is in front of the user with spatial cues and warns only on real physical hazards, and voice-dialed calling matches spoken names across scripts before handing off to the phone dialer.
  • Three-layer speech fallback so the agent is never mute, with vision answers returned as structured JSON and spoken uncertainty first for safety, deployed on Replit with server-side rate limiting and 46 passing tests.
